    Chapter 5: Computer Networks

    5.1 Introduction to Networking

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    network n. 网络 A computer network connects multiple devices for data exchange. Foundation of modern communication.
    communication n. 通信 Networks enable communication between computers. Key function of networking.
    data transmission n. 数据传输 Data transmission speed is measured in bits per second. Also called data transfer.
    connection n. 连接 The connection between computers is established through cables or wireless signals. Can be physical or logical.
    topology n. 拓扑结构 Network topology describes how devices are connected. Common types: star, bus, ring.
    protocol n. 协议 TCP/IP is the standard communication protocol on the Internet. Rules for data exchange.
    bandwidth n. 带宽 Higher bandwidth allows faster data transfer. Measured in bits per second.
    latency n. 延迟 Low latency is essential for real-time applications. Delay between sending and receiving.
    packet n. 数据包 Data are transmitted in small units called packets. Basic unit of network data.
    transmission medium n. 传输介质 Fiber optics are a high-speed transmission medium. Includes cables and radio waves.

    5.2 Types of Networks

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    LAN (Local Area Network) n. 局域网 LANs are used to connect computers within a building. Covers small geographic areas.
    WAN (Wide Area Network) n. 广域网 The Internet is the largest WAN in the world. Connects multiple LANs.
    MAN (Metropolitan Area Network) n. 城域网 A MAN connects networks within a city. Intermediate scale.
    PAN (Personal Area Network) n. 个人区域网 Bluetooth devices form a personal area network. Very short range.
    intranet n. 内联网 The company’s intranet is accessible only to employees. Private network.
    extranet n. 外联网 Business partners can access the extranet. Controlled external access.
    Internet n. 互联网 The Internet connects millions of networks worldwide. Global network.
    client-server model n. 客户端-服务器模型 Most online systems use the client-server model. Client requests, server responds.
    peer-to-peer (P2P) adj. 点对点 Peer-to-peer networks share files directly between users. Decentralized structure.
    virtual private network (VPN) n. 虚拟专用网络 VPNs provide secure remote access to private networks. Encrypts Internet traffic.

    5.3 Network Devices

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    router n. 路由器 A router connects different networks together. Directs network traffic.
    switch n. 交换机 Switches connect multiple devices within a LAN. Operates at data link layer.
    hub n. 集线器 Hubs broadcast data to all connected devices. Basic, outdated device.
    modem n. 调制解调器 A modem connects a computer to the Internet via phone lines. Converts signals.
    access point n. 接入点 The access point provides wireless Internet access. Wi-Fi hotspot.
    repeater n. 中继器 A repeater amplifies signals over long distances. Extends network range.
    bridge n. 网桥 A bridge connects two LAN segments. Operates at layer 2.
    gateway n. 网关 The gateway connects a local network to external networks. Entry/exit point of data.
    firewall n. 防火墙 A firewall filters network traffic for security. Hardware or software-based.
    NIC (Network Interface Card) n. 网络接口卡 Every computer needs a NIC to connect to a network. Hardware adapter.

    5.4 Network Media and Transmission

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    transmission medium n. 传输介质 Twisted-pair cables are common transmission media. Physical carrier.
    cable n. 电缆 Ethernet cables connect computers to routers. e.g., Cat5e, Cat6.
    fiber optic cable n. 光纤电缆 Fiber optics offer the fastest data transmission. Uses light signals.
    coaxial cable n. 同轴电缆 Coaxial cables are used in cable television networks. Copper-based.
    wireless adj. 无线的 Wireless technology uses radio waves instead of cables. Includes Wi-Fi, Bluetooth.
    radio wave n. 无线电波 Wi-Fi transmits data through radio waves. Frequency-based.
    signal n. 信号 The router sends digital signals over the network. Carries encoded data.
    interference n. 干扰 Electronic devices may cause wireless interference. Affects transmission quality.
    frequency n. 频率 Wi-Fi operates at 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz frequency bands. Measured in hertz (Hz).
    bandwidth n. 带宽 Higher bandwidth supports faster communication. Transmission capacity.

    5.5 Network Protocols

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    protocol n. 协议 Protocols define the rules for data communication. Standardized procedures.
    TCP/IP n. 传输控制协议/网际协议 TCP/IP is the foundation of the Internet. Fundamental suite of protocols.
    HTTP (Hypertext Transfer Protocol) n. 超文本传输协议 HTTP is used for transferring web pages. Web protocol.
    HTTPS (Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure) n. 安全超文本传输协议 HTTPS encrypts communication between browsers and servers. Secure version of HTTP.
    FTP (File Transfer Protocol) n. 文件传输协议 FTP is used to upload and download files. Common for file sharing.
    SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) n. 简单邮件传输协议 SMTP sends email messages over the Internet. Works with POP3/IMAP.
    DNS (Domain Name System) n. 域名系统 DNS translates domain names into IP addresses. Internet “phone book.”
    IP address n. IP 地址 Every device on the Internet has a unique IP address. IPv4 or IPv6 format.
    MAC address n. MAC 地址 The MAC address identifies a device’s network card. Hardware identifier.
    DHCP (Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol) n. 动态主机配置协议 DHCP assigns IP addresses automatically. Simplifies network setup.

    5.6 Internet and Web Technologies

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    Internet n. 互联网 The Internet connects computers worldwide. Global network.
    World Wide Web (WWW) n. 万维网 The Web is a collection of interlinked web pages. Built on HTTP.
    website n. 网站 The company launched a new website last week. Collection of web pages.
    web page n. 网页 Each web page is written in HTML. Basic unit of the web.
    hyperlink n. 超链接 Click the hyperlink to open another page. Connects documents.
    domain name n. 域名 “openai.com” is a domain name. Translated by DNS.
    URL (Uniform Resource Locator) n. 统一资源定位符 The URL identifies the location of a web page. e.g., https://example.com
    browser n. 浏览器 Chrome is one of the most popular web browsers. Accesses web content.
    search engine n. 搜索引擎 Google is the world’s leading search engine. Finds web information.
    download v./n. 下载 You can download files from the Internet. Opposite: upload.

    5.7 Network Security

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    security n. 安全 Network security protects data from unauthorized access. Broad concept.
    encryption n. 加密 Data encryption prevents interception. Converts plaintext to ciphertext.
    decryption n. 解密 Decryption converts encrypted data back to readable form. Reverse of encryption.
    authentication n. 身份验证 Authentication verifies the user’s identity. Step before access.
    authorization n. 授权 Authorization grants permission to users. Determines allowed actions.
    firewall n. 防火墙 A firewall filters incoming and outgoing traffic. Hardware/software barrier.
    malware n. 恶意软件 Malware includes viruses, worms, and spyware. Malicious programs.
    virus n. 计算机病毒 A virus can damage files and systems. Needs a host program.
    hacker n. 黑客 Hackers may attempt to break into systems. Can be ethical or malicious.
    antivirus n. 防病毒软件 Install antivirus software to protect your system. Detects and removes malware.

    5.8 Wireless and Mobile Networking

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    wireless network n. 无线网络 The office uses a wireless network for convenience. Wi-Fi-based.
    Wi-Fi n. 无线局域网 Wi-Fi enables Internet access without cables. Uses radio frequencies.
    hotspot n. 无线热点 Free Wi-Fi hotspots are available in cafes. Public access point.
    Bluetooth n. 蓝牙 Bluetooth connects wireless devices over short distances. Short-range technology.
    mobile network n. 移动网络 5G mobile networks offer high-speed Internet. Cellular communication.
    cellular adj. 蜂窝的 Cellular technology powers mobile phone networks. Based on radio cells.
    satellite communication n. 卫星通信 Satellites provide global Internet coverage. Long-distance data link.
    signal strength n. 信号强度 Weak signal strength reduces connection stability. Measured in dBm.
    coverage n. 覆盖范围 5G provides wider coverage and faster speed. Network area availability.
    roaming n. 漫游 Roaming allows mobile devices to use foreign networks. International access.

    5.9 Emerging Network Technologies

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    IoT (Internet of Things) n. 物联网 IoT connects everyday devices to the Internet. Smart homes, sensors, etc.
    cloud computing n. 云计算 Cloud computing delivers services over the Internet. Includes storage and processing.
    edge computing n. 边缘计算 Edge computing processes data near the source. Reduces latency.
    virtualization n. 虚拟化 Virtualization allows multiple OS to run on one machine. Used in servers.
    data center n. 数据中心 Large companies store data in secure data centers. Physical infrastructure.
    5G n. 第五代移动通信技术 5G provides ultra-fast wireless connectivity. Successor to 4G LTE.
    smart device n. 智能设备 Smart devices can connect and communicate automatically. e.g., smart TVs, watches.
    network automation n. 网络自动化 Automation reduces human intervention in network management. Emerging IT trend.
    SDN (Software Defined Networking) n. 软件定义网络 SDN separates control from hardware in networks. Increases flexibility.
    cybersecurity n. 网络安全 Cybersecurity focuses on protecting systems from digital attacks. Expanding field of IT.
