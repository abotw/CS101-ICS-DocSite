    Chapter 2: Computer Hardware

    2.1 Overview of Computer Hardware

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    hardware n. 硬件 Hardware refers to the physical parts of a computer. Opposite of software.
    component n. 组件，部件 Each hardware component has a specific function. Includes CPU, memory, storage, etc.
    motherboard n. 主板 All major components are connected to the motherboard. Also called mainboard.
    system unit n. 主机箱 The system unit houses the main hardware components. Sometimes called chassis.
    case n. 机箱 The case protects the internal components. Metal or plastic enclosure.
    slot n. 插槽 Memory modules are inserted into slots on the motherboard. Expansion or memory slot.
    port n. 接口，端口 USB ports are used to connect external devices. Types: USB, HDMI, Ethernet.
    cable n. 电缆 Connect the monitor with an HDMI cable. Transmission medium.
    connector n. 连接器 The cable connector must match the port type. Physical plug.
    power supply unit (PSU) n. 电源供应器 The PSU converts AC to DC for computer components. Provides stable voltage.

    2.2 The Central Processing Unit (CPU)

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    CPU (Central Processing Unit) n. 中央处理器 The CPU executes program instructions. “The brain of the computer.”
    processor n. 处理器 The processor speed is measured in gigahertz. Synonym of CPU.
    core n. 核心 A multi-core processor can handle several tasks at once. e.g., dual-core, quad-core.
    clock speed n. 时钟速度 Clock speed determines how fast the CPU operates. Measured in GHz.
    cache n. 高速缓存 Cache memory stores frequently used data for quick access. Levels: L1, L2, L3.
    register n. 寄存器 Registers store temporary data inside the CPU. Very small, high-speed memory.
    ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) n. 算术逻辑单元 The ALU performs arithmetic and logical operations. Core component of CPU.
    control unit n. 控制单元 The control unit directs the CPU’s activities. Coordinates CPU operations.
    instruction set n. 指令集 Each CPU has its own instruction set architecture. e.g., x86, ARM.
    heat sink n. 散热片 The heat sink helps to cool the processor. Prevents overheating.

    2.3 Memory and Storage

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    memory n. 内存，存储器 The computer temporarily stores data in memory. Usually volatile (RAM).
    RAM (Random Access Memory) n. 随机存取存储器 RAM is cleared when the power is off. Volatile memory.
    ROM (Read Only Memory) n. 只读存储器 ROM stores permanent system instructions. Non-volatile memory.
    main memory n. 主存储器 Programs are loaded into main memory before execution. Refers to RAM + cache.
    secondary storage n. 辅助存储 Hard drives are secondary storage devices. Non-volatile storage.
    hard disk drive (HDD) n. 硬盘驱动器 HDDs use magnetic disks to store data. Older but cheaper storage.
    solid-state drive (SSD) n. 固态硬盘 SSDs are faster and more reliable than HDDs. No moving parts.
    flash memory n. 闪存 Flash memory is used in USB drives and cameras. Non-volatile storage.
    optical disk n. 光盘 CDs and DVDs are types of optical disks. Read using a laser.
    capacity n. 容量 The storage capacity is measured in gigabytes or terabytes. Common units: GB, TB.

    2.4 Input Devices

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    input device n. 输入设备 A keyboard is a common input device. Devices for entering data.
    keyboard n. 键盘 Users type data with the keyboard. Standard layout: QWERTY.
    mouse n. 鼠标 The mouse controls the cursor on the screen. Optical or wireless.
    touchpad n. 触控板 Laptops have a built-in touchpad for navigation. Replaces a mouse.
    scanner n. 扫描仪 The scanner converts printed text into digital form. Optical input device.
    microphone n. 麦克风 The microphone captures sound for recording. Converts sound to digital data.
    webcam n. 网络摄像头 A webcam records live video. Used for online meetings.
    joystick n. 操纵杆 A joystick is used for controlling games. Analog input device.
    touch screen n. 触摸屏 Tablets use touch screens for input and control. Detects touch signals.
    sensor n. 传感器 Sensors detect physical conditions like light or motion. Used in IoT devices.

    2.5 Output Devices

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    output device n. 输出设备 Monitors and printers are output devices. Show or produce results.
    monitor n. 显示器 The monitor displays text and images. Types: LCD, LED.
    display n. 显示屏 The laptop display has a resolution of 1920×1080. General term for screen.
    printer n. 打印机 The printer produces paper copies of documents. Inkjet or laser.
    speaker n. 扬声器 Speakers output sound from the computer. Audio output device.
    projector n. 投影仪 A projector displays images on a large screen. Output for presentations.
    resolution n. 分辨率 High resolution produces clearer images. e.g., 4K, 1080p.
    pixel n. 像素 Each image is made up of millions of pixels. Smallest unit on a screen.
    refresh rate n. 刷新率 The monitor’s refresh rate is 144 Hz. Frequency of image updates.
    3D printer n. 三维打印机 3D printers create objects from digital models. Produces physical objects.

    2.6 Storage Media and Data Access

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    storage device n. 存储设备 USB drives are portable storage devices. Used for saving files.
    USB drive n. U盘 A USB drive stores data in flash memory. Also called thumb drive.
    memory card n. 存储卡 Cameras use memory cards to store photos. Types: SD, microSD.
    CD-ROM n. 光盘只读存储器 A CD-ROM can only be read, not written. Outdated media.
    DVD n. 数字视频光盘 A DVD can store about 4.7 GB of data. Optical medium.
    Blu-ray disc n. 蓝光光盘 Blu-ray discs offer higher storage capacity. Used for HD video.
    external drive n. 外置硬盘 External drives are connected via USB. Portable backup device.
    access time n. 访问时间 SSDs have lower access times than HDDs. Time to retrieve data.
    data transfer rate n. 数据传输速率 The USB 3.0 transfer rate is up to 5 Gbps. Also called bandwidth.
    format v./n. 格式化；格式 Please format the disk before use. Erases all data.

    2.7 Expansion and Connectivity

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    expansion card n. 扩展卡 You can add a graphics card to improve performance. Plug-in component.
    graphics card n. 显卡 The graphics card handles image rendering. GPU-based.
    sound card n. 声卡 The sound card processes audio input and output. Enhances audio quality.
    network card n. 网卡 The network card connects the computer to a network. Wired or wireless.
    slot n. 插槽 Expansion cards are inserted into PCI slots. On motherboard.
    connector n. 接头，连接器 Make sure the connector fits the port. Physical interface.
    USB (Universal Serial Bus) n. 通用串行总线 USB ports support many devices. Common interface standard.
    HDMI (High-Definition Multimedia Interface) n. 高清多媒体接口 HDMI cables carry video and audio signals. Display connection.
    Ethernet n. 以太网 Ethernet is the most common wired network technology. LAN standard.
    wireless adj. 无线的 Wireless connections use radio signals. e.g., Wi-Fi, Bluetooth.

    2.8 Power and Maintenance

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    power supply n. 电源 The power supply provides electricity to all components. Abbr: PSU.
    voltage n. 电压 The PSU converts AC voltage to DC. Measured in volts (V).
    cable management n. 线缆管理 Proper cable management improves airflow. Good maintenance habit.
    fan n. 风扇 The CPU fan keeps the processor cool. Cooling system.
    heat sink n. 散热片 Heat sinks absorb heat from chips. Often made of aluminum.
    maintenance n. 维护 Regular maintenance prevents hardware failure. Includes cleaning and inspection.
    failure n. 故障 Hardware failure may cause data loss. e.g., power failure.
    replace v. 更换 You should replace the battery if it’s damaged. Common maintenance action.
    upgrade v. 升级 Users can upgrade RAM to improve performance. Also applies to software.
    warranty n. 保修 The computer comes with a one-year warranty. Manufacturer guarantee.
