    Chapter 11: Computer Ethics and Society

    11.1 Introduction to Computer Ethics

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    ethics n. 道德；伦理 Computer ethics concerns responsible technology use. Guides right and wrong behavior in computing.
    morality n. 道德观 Morality refers to principles that define good and bad conduct. Broader than ethics.
    responsibility n. 责任 Developers have a responsibility to ensure software safety. Ethical and professional duty.
    integrity n. 诚信 Maintaining data integrity is both technical and ethical. Also means moral honesty.
    honesty n. 诚实 Honesty in data reporting builds trust in research. Core ethical value.
    transparency n. 透明度 Transparency helps users understand how data are used. Required in AI and data handling.
    accountability n. 问责 Organizations must take accountability for system failures. Tied to responsibility.
    fairness n. 公平 AI fairness ensures that algorithms do not discriminate. Ethical AI goal.
    bias n. 偏见 Biased algorithms can lead to unfair results. Ethical issue in data-driven systems.
    trust n. 信任 Users must trust that systems protect their privacy. Foundation of digital society.

    11.2 Privacy and Data Protection

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    privacy n. 隐私 Online privacy is a growing concern in the digital age. Right to control personal information.
    data protection n. 数据保护 Data protection laws regulate how data are collected and stored. Includes GDPR and similar acts.
    personal data n. 个人数据 Personal data include names, addresses, and biometric info. Must be protected from misuse.
    consent n. 同意 Websites must obtain user consent before collecting data. Legal requirement in most regions.
    anonymity n. 匿名 Anonymity protects user identity online. Important in research and social media.
    surveillance n. 监控 Mass surveillance threatens privacy rights. Ethical and political issue.
    data breach n. 数据泄露 The company apologized for the recent data breach. Unauthorized data exposure.
    encryption n. 加密 Encryption helps protect private communication. Technical safeguard.
    confidentiality n. 保密性 Doctors and lawyers must maintain client confidentiality. Ethical and legal duty.
    GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) n. 通用数据保护条例 GDPR enforces strong privacy protection in the EU. European privacy law.

    11.3 Intellectual Property and Copyright

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    intellectual property (IP) n. 知识产权 Intellectual property protects creative and technical works. Includes patents, trademarks, copyrights.
    copyright n. 版权 Software is protected under copyright law. Grants exclusive rights to creators.
    patent n. 专利 Patents protect new inventions for a limited time. Encourages innovation.
    trademark n. 商标 A company’s logo is protected by trademark law. Symbol of brand identity.
    piracy n. 盗版 Software piracy violates intellectual property laws. Illegal duplication or distribution.
    plagiarism n. 抄袭 Plagiarism is copying someone else’s work without credit. Serious academic offense.
    open source n. 开源 Open-source software allows free use and modification. Opposite of proprietary software.
    license n. 许可证 You must agree to the license before using the software. Defines usage rights.
    proprietary software n. 专有软件 Proprietary software is owned and controlled by a company. Not open to modification.
    fair use n. 合理使用 Fair use allows limited use of copyrighted materials. Legal exception for education and research.

    11.4 Digital Citizenship

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    digital citizenship n. 数字公民素养 Digital citizenship promotes responsible online behavior. Includes ethics, safety, and respect.
    digital literacy n. 数字素养 Digital literacy helps users evaluate online information critically. Skill for the modern age.
    online behavior n. 网络行为 Responsible online behavior is essential for safety. Also called digital etiquette.
    netiquette n. 网络礼仪 Netiquette means showing respect in online communication. Internet + etiquette.
    cyberbullying n. 网络欺凌 Cyberbullying can cause serious emotional harm. Online harassment.
    misinformation n. 错误信息 Misinformation spreads quickly through social media. Often unintentional.
    disinformation n. 虚假信息 Disinformation is deliberately false or misleading content. Intentional deception.
    digital footprint n. 数字足迹 Every online action leaves a digital footprint. Record of user activity.
    social responsibility n. 社会责任 Tech companies have a social responsibility to prevent misuse. Ethical obligation.
    cyber ethics n. 网络伦理 Cyber ethics define acceptable behavior in digital environments. Core of digital citizenship.

    11.5 Professional Ethics in Computing

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    professionalism n. 职业精神 Professionalism includes honesty, skill, and accountability. Essential for IT workers.
    code of conduct n. 行为准则 Many organizations have a code of conduct for employees. Defines professional standards.
    confidentiality n. 保密义务 Developers must maintain user confidentiality. Ethical and contractual duty.
    conflict of interest n. 利益冲突 Conflicts of interest must be disclosed by professionals. Ethical transparency.
    whistleblower n. 吹哨人 Whistleblowers expose unethical practices within organizations. Protected by law in some regions.
    misuse n. 滥用 Misuse of data can lead to legal consequences. Opposite of proper use.
    negligence n. 过失 Negligence in system design can endanger users. Legal responsibility.
    accountability n. 责任制 Accountability ensures that developers own their work’s consequences. Key in safety-critical systems.
    ethical dilemma n. 伦理困境 Engineers often face ethical dilemmas in decision-making. No clear right answer.
    professional association n. 专业协会 ACM and IEEE promote ethical standards in computing. Maintain professional integrity.

    11.6 Technology and Society

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    digital divide n. 数字鸿沟 The digital divide separates those with and without technology access. Social inequality issue.
    accessibility n. 无障碍性 Accessibility ensures everyone can use technology equally. Inclusive design goal.
    inclusion n. 包容 Digital inclusion promotes equal opportunities online. Related to accessibility.
    automation n. 自动化 Automation changes the nature of employment. Can displace workers.
    unemployment n. 失业 Technology may lead to short-term unemployment. Ethical and economic issue.
    surveillance society n. 监控社会 Critics warn of a growing surveillance society. Privacy concern.
    social media n. 社交媒体 Social media influences political and cultural life. Double-edged impact.
    digital democracy n. 数字民主 Digital democracy allows citizens to participate online. e-Government systems.
    freedom of speech n. 言论自由 The Internet provides a platform for freedom of speech. Also requires regulation.
    human rights n. 人权 Access to the Internet is seen as a basic human right. Supported by the UN.

    11.7 Ethics of Emerging Technologies

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    AI ethics n. 人工智能伦理 AI ethics focus on fairness, transparency, and accountability. Key concern in modern AI.
    algorithmic bias n. 算法偏见 Algorithmic bias can lead to discrimination in AI systems. Ethical challenge.
    automation ethics n. 自动化伦理 Automation ethics examine the human impact of replacing workers. Balances progress and humanity.
    data ethics n. 数据伦理 Data ethics promote responsible collection and use of information. Protects privacy and integrity.
    bioethics n. 生物伦理 Bioethics addresses issues like genetic engineering. Technology in medicine.
    surveillance ethics n. 监控伦理 Surveillance ethics balance security with individual privacy. Political and legal aspect.
    digital autonomy n. 数字自主 Digital autonomy means individuals control their online presence. Empowerment concept.
    consent management n. 同意管理 Consent management ensures users control their personal data. GDPR principle.
    transparency principle n. 透明原则 Transparency is crucial for building ethical AI systems. One of core AI ethics.
    sustainability ethics n. 可持续伦理 Sustainability ethics guide eco-friendly tech development. Aligns technology with environment.

    11.8 Global and Legal Aspects

    Word Part of Speech Chinese Meaning Example Sentence Note
    regulation n. 监管 Governments introduce new regulations for data protection. Legal control.
    legislation n. 立法 Technology legislation evolves to meet digital challenges. Laws and acts.
    compliance n. 合规 Companies must ensure compliance with national cybersecurity laws. Legal adherence.
    jurisdiction n. 管辖权 Jurisdiction issues arise in cross-border cybercrimes. International law concept.
    intellectual property law n. 知识产权法 Intellectual property law protects creative works globally. Includes copyright and patents.
    cyber law n. 网络法 Cyber law governs online behavior and transactions. Covers privacy and e-commerce.
    digital rights n. 数字权利 Digital rights protect freedom and access in the online world. Part of human rights.
    international cooperation n. 国际合作 Cybersecurity requires international cooperation. Global joint effort.
    data sovereignty n. 数据主权 Data sovereignty means data are subject to local laws. Related to cloud storage.
    global governance n. 全球治理 Global governance is needed for ethical technology development. Policy-level concern.
